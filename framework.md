# Innovation Management Framework

A comprehensive approach to building innovation capacity through strategic design and tactical execution.

## Solution Structure

**Modular Work Units:** Self-contained building blocks you can plug into existing workflows or run as standalone sprints.

- **Inputs:** Key documents, concepts, and tools such as strategic plans, customer personas, and ideation templates
- **Tasks:** Autonomous exercises (pre-recorded videos and guided worksheets) and guided workshops
- **Outputs:** Tangible deliverables like opportunity maps, idea scorecards, and prototype schematics

## Process Flow

1. **Self-Diagnostic (AI-Guided):** Online chatbot questionnaire assessing current innovation capabilities
2. **Initial Diagnosis:** Facilitated discovery session mapping existing processes and pain points
3. **Idea Generation & Evaluation:** Guided brainstorming followed by weighted scoring
4. **Prototype Conceptualization:** Rapid design sprint to sketch and storyboard key features
5. **Validation & Feedback:** User interviews and usability tests
6. **Iteration & Refinement:** Continuous improvement through lean cycles

## Methodologies

- 24 Steps of Disciplined Entrepreneurship
- Lean Startup (Build–Measure–Learn)
- Running Lean (Rapid hypothesis validation)
- Design Thinking (Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test)
